# 🎉 Guest Mode - Free Trial (1 Month)

Try Sea completely free for 1 month! No login required.

## How It Works

### ✅ First Month (Free)
- No account needed
- Use Sea however you want
- All your questions & chats are saved automatically 💾
- **Usage tracked:** See how many questions you've asked

### ⏰ After 1 Month
If you're still using guest mode:
1. **Automatic logout** 🔒
2. Can't access app until you login/register
3. All your data is still there (saved!) 
4. Just login to continue!

### 🔓 Logging In After Trial
When you login or register:
- Use your email
- All your previous questions appear
- Your learning history is preserved
- Continue where you left off!

## 🎯 Why Do This?

The 1-month trial is designed to:
- ✅ Let you test Sea risk-free
- ✅ See if you like it
- ✅ Then create an account to keep using
- ✅ Keep your learning history forever

After 1 month, we ask you to create an account because:
- 🔐 Real accounts are more secure
- 📧 Email login is more reliable than guest mode
- 💾 Account data is backed up and protected
- 👤 Personal learning history tied to YOU

---

## 📌 What's Saved in Guest Mode?

✅ All your questions  
✅ All your learning conversations  
✅ How many questions you've asked  
✅ Your age group (for appropriate content)  

❌ NOT saved:  
- Account info (that's added when you login)
- Password (you set this when registering)

---

## 🔄 Guest Data Transfer

When you login after the trial:

```
Guest Mode Data                Account Data
├─ All questions        ─→  Linked to your email
├─ All conversations    ─→  Saved in your account
├─ Learning history     ─→  Preserved forever
└─ Usage stats          ─→  Kept for reference
```

**Your data doesn't disappear!** It just gets linked to a real account.

---

## ⚠️ What Happens If You Don't Login?

After 1 month as guest:

1. **You log out (forced)**
2. **Can't access the app** unless you login/register
3. **Data isn't deleted** - it's stored
4. **If you login later** - All your old questions are there!

💡 **Tip:** It's not gone, just inaccessible until you create an account.
